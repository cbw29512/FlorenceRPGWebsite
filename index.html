Table formation, not social media

The system should get people to a game—not keep them chatting in an app.

Matching will use structured preferences and verified table information. No public member directory, no direct messages, and no exposing private contact details to strangers.

  1. 1

    Tell us what fits

    System, role, experience, availability, travel range, campaign length, play style, and accessibility needs.

  2. 2

    Filter first

    Age, schedule, system, capacity, venue, accessibility, and safety requirements must be compatible before scoring.

  3. 3

    Form a table

    Compatible people receive the same structured invitation with the game, time, expectations, and confirmed venue.

  4. 4

    Go play

    Accept, decline, withdraw, or report. The Guild does not need a private-chat layer to organize the session.

The participation boundary

Adults match as individuals. Youth participate as groups.

Individual matching is 18+. A group with players under 18 can make a separate inquiry when a parent or guardian provides consent. Youth groups never enter the adult matching pool.

18+

Adult matching

Individual player/GM/Keeper matching is limited to adults.

◇

Youth groups

An existing youth group can participate through a parent/guardian inquiry and consent process.

◆

Young GMs

A young DM/GM can be trained in an appropriate supervised group setting instead of being privately paired with adults.

Verified places

Quick answers

New to tabletop RPGs?

Do I need books, dice, or a character?

No. Beginner sessions can use pregenerated characters and shared materials, and the Guild links to a free dice roller.

What is a one-shot?

A complete adventure designed to begin and end in one session. It is one of the easiest ways to try a game without committing to a campaign.

Can I sign up as both a player and a DM or Keeper?

Yes. Adult members can select both roles.

Can someone under 18 sign up individually?

No. Individual matching is 18+. A parent or guardian can submit an existing youth group separately, with consent, for manual review.

Existing youth group?

Do not submit minors to the adult interest pool. A parent or legal guardian can submit a group inquiry for manual review. The Guild can help a young DM learn in an appropriate supervised setting; youth groups remain separate from adult individual matching.
