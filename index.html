How table formation will work

ZIP code and compatibility—not social browsing.

The national model is built around where people can reasonably travel and what kind of table they actually want.

Submit preferences

System, role, ZIP code, travel radius, schedule, experience, campaign format, play style, and accessibility needs.

Filter hard conflicts

Age, system, schedule, capacity, venue, accessibility, and safety rules must fit before scoring begins.

Receive a table invite

The same structured game information goes to each candidate. Contact details stay private.

Accept and play

Fill the seats, confirm the venue, and get to the table. No public profile browsing or private stranger messaging required.

The backend will not invent activity.

When the Guild Hall eventually shows player counts, open seats, chapters, events, or venues, those values will come from actual records. Until then, the site will say that matching is forming rather than display fake momentum.

Parent / guardian path

Existing youth group?

Do not submit minors to the adult interest pool. A parent or legal guardian can submit an existing group for manual review. Youth groups remain separate from adult individual matching, and a young DM can receive appropriate supervised training.

Youth-group requests are reviewed manually and never enter adult automatic matching.
