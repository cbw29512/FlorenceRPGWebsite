Before your first session

The questions beginners are usually afraid to ask.

Do I need books or dice?

No. A beginner table can provide what you need and use pregenerated characters.

Do I have to act?

No. Speak as your character or simply describe what they do. Both are roleplaying.

What if I do the wrong thing?

There usually is not one correct answer. Tell the DM what you want to attempt and the game responds.

What does the DM do?

The DM describes the world, plays its characters, presents challenges, and adjudicates the rules. The DM is not your opponent.

Quick answers

New to tabletop RPGs?

What is a tabletop role-playing game?

A group tells a story together. Players decide what their characters attempt, a Game Master describes the world and consequences, and dice help resolve uncertain outcomes.

What is a one-shot?

A complete adventure designed to begin and end in one session. It is one of the easiest ways to try a new game without committing to a campaign.

Can I sign up as both a player and a DM or Keeper?

Yes. Choose both roles below. Growing more local storytellers is one of the best ways to create more tables.

Is Call of Cthulhu just D&D with horror?

No. D&D centers heroic fantasy and tactical adventure. Call of Cthulhu emphasizes investigation, ordinary people, dangerous knowledge, and horror. The Florence site will teach them separately.
