More than an app directory

National reach. Local tables.

Light Tower started in Florence, South Carolina, but the Guild is designed for tabletop players across the United States. The website provides the tools and coordination; the goal is still to get people around an actual table.

The Guild standardUseful before impressive.

Every project should help somebody learn, prepare, run, or play. Live, Beta, Coming Soon, and Workshop labels tell you what is genuinely ready.

Community-supported · no ads

Help keep the light on.

Light Tower does not run advertisements or paid placements. If the Guild saves your table time, optional support helps cover hosting, original adventures, learning resources, and new tools.
