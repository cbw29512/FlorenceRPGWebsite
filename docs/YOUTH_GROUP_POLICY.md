# Light Tower Table Top Guild — Youth Group Policy

## Core rule

Light Tower Table Top Guild individual player/GM matching is **18+**.

A person under 18 is not entered into the individual matching pool and is never paired one-to-one with an unknown adult through the Guild.

## Allowed youth path

Youth participation is allowed only when all of these are true:

1. The youth are participating as an **existing group**, not as individually discoverable profiles.
2. A parent or legal guardian submits the group request and provides consent for every minor participating, or each participating minor has documented parent/guardian consent before the group is approved.
3. The group has a clearly identified responsible adult/guardian contact for scheduling and operational communication.
4. The game takes place at a verified public/community venue or another location specifically approved for the youth group by the responsible guardians. The Guild does not publish private home addresses.
5. Youth participants are not given direct-message access to adult Guild members.
6. Adult volunteers are not privately matched with individual minors.
7. Any adult support, instruction, or GM mentoring happens in a group/supervised context with guardian knowledge.

## Young Game Masters

Light Tower Table Top Guild may help a young person learn to become a Dungeon Master/Game Master/Keeper.

The preferred youth model is to **teach the young GM how to run their own group** rather than assigning an unrelated adult to become the group's regular GM.

For the current Guild operating model:

- The Guild founder may train or mentor a young DM/GM in an appropriate group/supervised setting.
- The Guild founder does **not** serve as the regular DM for a youth group.
- Youth-group games should be run by the group's own designated GM/DM/Keeper or through a separately verified supervised youth program.

## Data boundary

Youth records should be group-level wherever possible.

A youth-group request may store:
- parent/guardian name and contact information;
- approximate participant count;
- age range rather than unnecessary exact birth dates;
- chosen game/system;
- beginner experience level;
- accessibility requirements;
- preferred dates/times;
- whether the group already has a young GM/DM/Keeper;
- whether GM training is requested;
- guardian-consent status and verification timestamp.

The system should not create publicly searchable profiles for minors.

## Communication boundary

Allowed:
- parent/guardian ↔ Guild operational communication;
- group-wide event notices;
- structured RSVP/cancellation notices;
- supervised GM-training coordination;
- safety/report forms.

Not allowed:
- adult ↔ minor private messaging;
- public youth profiles;
- youth location sharing;
- adult discovery/search of minors;
- individual minor matching;
- anonymous chat rooms.

## Implementation rule

The website must present two distinct paths:

- **Adult (18+) — player / GM / Keeper matching**
- **Parent/guardian — existing youth group inquiry**

A youth group inquiry is reviewed manually and never enters the automatic adult matching algorithm.
