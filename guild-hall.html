Where tables come together

Welcome to the Guild Hall.

This is where Light Tower turns interest into real tables: Game Masters, players, local chapters, verified places to play, beginner sessions, and original adventures. The goal is simple—help people meet safely and actually play.

What the Guild Hall does

Community activity with a purpose.

Light Tower's active learning and organizer support currently focuses on Dungeons & Dragons and Call of Cthulhu. Other tabletop RPGs can be recorded as interest and shared as information, but the Guild does not promise organized support for every system.

Places to play

Verified venues only

Businesses, libraries, game stores, restaurants, breweries, and community spaces remain candidates until a human verifies permission and current details.

How chapters verify venues →
No fabricated community numbers.

If there are zero confirmed tables in an area, the Guild will say zero. When live counts appear here, they will come from stored records with timestamps—not marketing placeholders.

How a table forms

Structured coordination instead of direct messages.

Members should not need to expose their contact information to strangers just to find a game.

1 · Preferences

Tell us what fits

Role, system, ZIP code, travel radius, schedule, experience, campaign type, play style, accessibility needs, and table preferences.

2 · Eligibility

Filter impossible matches

Age, schedule, system, capacity, venue, accessibility, moderation rules, and other hard requirements are checked first.

3 · Invitation

Propose a real table

Selected people receive the same structured invitation with the game, date, time, expectations, and verified venue.

4 · Play

Accept and show up

Accept, decline, withdraw, or report. Operational updates can go to the whole table without creating a private-chat network.

No chapter required

ZIP-radius matching

People outside an active chapter can still submit national interest. The matching model is designed around ZIP code and travel radius rather than forcing every user into a city-specific site.

Participation rules

Clear boundaries before growth.

Adults

Individual player / DM / Keeper matching is 18+. No public contact details, searchable member profiles, or adult-to-minor individual matching.

Youth groups

Players under 18 participate only as an existing group through a parent/guardian inquiry and consent process. Young DMs can receive appropriate supervised training without entering the adult matching pool.

Table conduct

Respect consent, personal boundaries, and the people at the table. Harassment, threats, hate speech, unwanted sexual content, and pressure to stay in an uncomfortable game are not acceptable.

Reporting

Participants can withdraw from or report a table, event, organizer, or venue through structured Guild tools without confronting another member privately. Safety concerns take priority over keeping a table together.
