Ask & answer

Tabletop questions first

Rules, character choices, DM problems, new-player questions and useful table stories have a home here.

Moderated

Submissions wait for Light Tower review before they become visible. Approved replies can be marked as a DM Answer.

The Board does not expose member emails, phone numbers or private matching information, and it does not offer stranger-to-stranger direct messages.
