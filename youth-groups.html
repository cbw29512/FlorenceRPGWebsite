Parent / guardian path

Existing youth group? Keep the group together.

Youth participation is handled separately from adult matching. A parent, guardian, or authorized responsible adult can submit an existing group for manual review, supervised support, or young-DM training.

No minor profilesNo adult/minor private matchingGuardian consentManual review

Youth group inquiry

Tell us about the group.

The Guild founder may help train a young DM/GM in an appropriate supervised/group setting but does not serve as the regular DM for a youth group.

Collect only what is needed at this stage. Do not enter individual minor names, phone numbers, social handles, or private home addresses.

Youth-group requests are reviewed manually and never enter adult automatic matching.

How this differs from adult matching

No youth discovery pool.

Light Tower does not create searchable profiles for minors or pair individual minors with unknown adults. The unit of participation is the existing group, with guardian knowledge and consent.
