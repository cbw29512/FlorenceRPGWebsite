The Guild Vault standard

Every adventure should arrive table-ready.

The goal is less prep and more actual play.

One complete ZIP

Adventure, characters, maps, handouts, and DM aids stay together.

Player / DM separation

Spoiler-safe material is clearly identified so reveals stay intact.

New-DM support

Adventures should explain how to recover when players surprise you, miss clues, debate rules, or slow the pace.

Real contents only

The page describes files actually in the bundle—no fake extras, ratings, or placeholder promises.
