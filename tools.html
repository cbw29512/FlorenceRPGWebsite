Tool policy

Use the tool. Come back to the Guild. Keep playing.

The tools are here to remove work from the table—not force everyone into one giant account or app.

Clear status

We say what is ready

Live tools are labeled live. Preview tools are labeled preview. Development projects stay in the workshop until they are useful enough to share.

Privacy

Your Guild information stays separate

Opening a tool does not automatically share your table-matching information, email address, or location with another project.

Table first

Every tool earns its place

If a tool does not help someone learn, prepare, run, or play a tabletop game, it does not need a spot on the public Tool Bench.

Community-supported · no ads

Help build better table tools.

There are no advertisements or paid placements on the Tool Bench. If a tool saves you time, optional support helps cover hosting and continued development.
