Why this stays useful

One Guild. Focused apps.

Light Tower is the hub, not another giant application. Tools can evolve independently while sharing clear branding, release expectations, and a path back here.

Status

You know what is ready

Live, Beta, Coming Soon, and Workshop mean different things and are enforced in validation.

Privacy

Your Guild data stays separate

Opening a tool does not automatically send your matching information, email, or location to another project.

Focus

Apps earn their place

A public Guild tool should make learning, preparation, running, or playing a tabletop game easier.

Request a Guild app.

Requests are private planning input, not public posts. Name and email are optional.

Repeated requests can directly influence what moves into the Workshop next.
