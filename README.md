# Florence Tabletop Guild

A responsive, accessibility-focused community website for tabletop role-playing games in Florence and the Pee Dee region of South Carolina.

## Current preview

The site highlights:

- Dungeons & Dragons and Call of Cthulhu
- Beginner-friendly and all-ages community pathways
- Game Master and Keeper resources
- Other TTRPGs coming soon, including Pathfinder, Starfinder, Blades in the Dark, Vaesen, and Cyberpunk RED
- Community safety and accessibility commitments
- Florence-focused SEO metadata and structured data
